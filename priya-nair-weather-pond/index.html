About the Researcher

Priya Nair grew up in Mississauga, downstream of everything, and got her start counting frogs on a school trip she was supposed to be supervising. She did a master's on constructed wetlands at Guelph, stayed for the ponds, and now splits her time between fieldwork for the city's stormwater program and the volunteer survey she cannot stop improving. She owns four pairs of waders, one good net, and strong opinions about storm drains, which she will share at very little provocation.
