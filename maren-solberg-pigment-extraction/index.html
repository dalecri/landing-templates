From a stone boathouse in Sandviken, Maren Solberg wild-harvests storm-cast kelp, fallen lichens, and iron-rich seepage clays around Bergen. Every pigment batch is washed in rainwater, hand-mulled on local granite slabs, and tested by paper chromatography before bottling.
