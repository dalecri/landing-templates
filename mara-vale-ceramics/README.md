# Mara Vale — Ceramics, Montréal

A ceramicist's portfolio reduced to the essentials: five forms, one at a time, each with a single quiet sentence.

## The persona (fictional)

**Mara Vale**, a ceramicist in Montréal making small-batch tableware and vessels: simple curves, earthy surfaces, and the small irregularities that let a handmade object feel at home. Five forms (Gathering bowl, Window vase, Evening plate, First cup, Still bottle). A pixel portrait stands in for a photo. Contact is a placeholder address (hello@maravale.example). Mara and her work are invented for this template.

## How the page works

- `index.html` is the whole template in one file (15,726 bytes). Only external call: Google Fonts (Space Mono). No weather, no canvas, no network data.
- **The form selector:** one vessel shown at a time from a five-form index, moved through with arrow controls; each form carries its number, name, and one line of description.
- The quietest page on the shelf: no hero-and-cards ladder, no pricing, nothing to sell but the work itself.

## Reusing it

1. Swap the persona: name, city, statement, and the five forms with their one-liners.
2. Keep the selector logic intact; forms are plain data in the page.
