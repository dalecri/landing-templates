01 / Work Experience

Work, in and out of the office

STATISTICS CANADA • 2018 — NOW

MEDIA RELATIONSSTATISTICS CANADA

Managing the Official Media Hotline

Challenge
On major economic release days, requests from media outlets across Canada arrive quickly. To meet our service standards, queries must be directed promptly, answered in the journalist's language of choice and vetted for media use.
What I do
As journalists' primary point of contact, I route their queries to certified experts and track each one to close, shaping the expert's answer into a well-suited reply that keeps the agency's professional tone without unnecessary technical jargon. I also lead embargoed lockups with national news agencies for releases like the Consumer Price Index (CPI), the Labour Force Survey (LFS) and gross domestic product (GDP).
Behind the scenes
I build internal dashboards, report on our key KPIs to management, certify experts for media interviews, and write the SOPs the team works from.
SOCIAL MEDIASTATISTICS CANADA

Social Media for Canada's National Statistical Agency

Challenge
Official statistics matter to all Canadians online, but no one enjoys parsing complex figures on social media.
What I did
Managed and helped grow the agency's channels, developed and ran campaigns for major data releases and survey collections, and coordinated live Reddit AMAs between subject matter experts and Canadians.
Behind the scenes
Developed performance reports for senior management using Meltwater, Hootsuite and platform analytics. I also wrote platform SOPs and trained new team members on social media best practices.
CONTENT BRAND@TWOSTORYTAILS

TwoStoryTails | Multicat + Bengal content

Challenge
Build a recognizable cat account in a crowded niche, with limited time to post.
What I did
Created the concept (four cats, two floors, two personalities), the visual identity and a posting rhythm. Then I built TwoStoryCaptions, a small tool that plans posts and drafts captions.
What changed
Faster content planning and a consistent brand voice across posts.
