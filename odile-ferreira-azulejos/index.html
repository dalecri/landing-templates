02 / Process

One tile, four turns

  1. Bisque

    Red clay from Alentejo, pressed and fired once to 1000°C.

  2. Glaze

    Dipped in tin-white. It stays chalky until the kiln.

  3. Pounce

    Charcoal dusted through a pricked paper stencil.

  4. Paint

    Cobalt oxide, one brush, one pass. Then fired again.
