# Cris Home — the owner's own homepage, living-sky edition

The odd one out on this shelf: not fictional, and not adapted from the ASCII collection. This is **Cristobal's own personal homepage** ("Cristobal · a quiet corner in Labelle"), shelved as the reference build of a masonhall-style personal page: one quiet scene, real weather, and a short page of work, projects, and cats.

## What's on the page

- A canvas scene of a house in the Laurentians (hills, stars, sun and moon, drifting clouds, lit windows) that wears **Labelle's real current weather**, fetched live from Open-Meteo: rain and snow fall on the house when they fall on Labelle.
- Plain personal-site sections, no glass-card ladder: an intro, **work**, **projects**, and **cats** (the four residents get their due), with real links out (StatCan, GitHub, LinkedIn, TwoStoryTails).
- Everything in one HTML file; the only external calls are the Open-Meteo fetch (no key) and any fonts it declares.

## Why it's here

The other five templates are fictional personas over collection engines. This one is the proof of intent: the style those templates borrow (a personal page with a living sky that tells the truth about the weather) applied to a real person, in production, as an actual homepage.

## Reusing it

1. It is a working homepage, not a blank: names, links, cats, and coordinates are the owner's. To make it yours, replace the identity, the section content, and the fetch coordinates (search the script for the Open-Meteo URL) with your own town.
2. The scene and the sections are independent: the canvas script can be lifted wholesale onto another page, and the sections read fine with the scene removed.
3. Kept in step with the version Cris uploads; this copy was added 2026-10-27 from `cris-home__1.html` (38,545 bytes).
