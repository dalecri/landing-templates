# Mara Ellison — Wood Kiln, Portland

A wood-fire ceramicist's portfolio whose headings are alive: kinetic type that stirs and scatters as the pointer passes through it, like ash off a kiln.

## The persona (fictional)

**Mara Ellison**, a ceramicist firing in a wood kiln on the edge of Portland, Oregon. She works with local clays and glazes made from the ash of the trees felled for the kiln, letting each piece record the heat of the firing and the weather that cools it. Six bodies of work: Douglas Fir Bowls, Willamette Plates, Night Firing Lamps, Ember Handles, the Quiet Set, and the Winter Kiln. Contact is a placeholder address (studio@maraellison.com). Mara and her work are invented for this template.

## How the page works

- `index.html` is the whole template in one file (6,563 bytes). Only external calls: Google Fonts (Space Mono) and the Open-Meteo fetch for live Portland weather (45.52, -122.68). No accounts, no tracking.
- **Kinetic type:** the featured technique. Headings and project titles are split into individual letters, and each letter eases away from the pointer within a small radius, settling back when it passes. The type behaves like the material: disturbed by whatever moves through it.
- One accent, a pale celadon green drawn from her crackled glazes, used sparingly on a dark ground.
- The work list is the page: six vessel families, each with a single line tying the piece back to ash, river, or weather.
