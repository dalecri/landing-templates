Vessels shaped by ash and the long cool of Portland rain.

Douglas Fir Bowls

Glaze from the ash of trees felled for the kiln.

Willamette Plates

Edges that follow the river’s curve.

Night Firing Lamps

Light held in crackled celadon.

Ember Handles

Pitchers whose grips remember the heat.

Quiet Set

Everyday vessels with quiet surfaces.

Winter Kiln

Forms cooled under the season’s first frost.

Mara Ellison fires her work in a wood kiln on the edge of Portland. She works with local clays and the ash that falls from the fire, letting each piece record the particular heat and the weather that cools it.
