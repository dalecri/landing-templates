01 / Work Experience

Work, in and out of the office

NORTHWIND STUDIO • 2021 — NOW

PRODUCT DESIGNNORTHWIND STUDIO

Designing a calmer analytics dashboard

Challenge
Independent hotels live inside their analytics dashboard, but the old one answered every question with a spreadsheet. Owners could not find occupancy, rates, or problem rooms without exporting a file and phoning support.
What I do
I lead design for the dashboard's core views: a morning briefing that answers 'how did we do, and what needs me today' on one screen, plus the design system underneath it. I pair with two engineers, run weekly owner interviews, and ship in small, reversible steps.
Behind the scenes
I maintain the component library and its docs, keep the research archive, track adoption and support-ticket themes as design KPIs, and write the release notes owners actually read.
CONTENT & COMMUNITYHARBOURLIGHT RETAIL

Community for a national outdoors retailer

Challenge
A beloved gear brand with a huge following, and a comments section full of unanswered questions about sizing, repairs, and where the snow was good.
What I did
Ran the brand's channels and community programs: a repair-stories series that became its most-shared content, seasonal trail guides, and live Q&As with guides and gear techs. Grew the community by keeping replies human and the posting rhythm sustainable.
Behind the scenes
Built the performance reporting and content calendar the team still runs on, from native analytics and a lot of spreadsheets. Wrote the voice guide and trained three community coordinators.
SIDE PROJECT@MARISOLMAKES

Marisol Makes | One tiny tool a month

Challenge
Keep a side practice alive next to a full-time job, without it eating the weekends it was meant to improve.
What I did
I ship one small, finished tool a month: a caption planner for tiny brands, a reading tracker, a terrarium timer. Each one gets a name, a one-page site, and a short write-up of what it taught me.
What changed
A portfolio that updates itself, and a steady trickle of freelance enquiries from people who found the tools first.
