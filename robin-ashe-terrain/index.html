01 · Hello

Hi, I'm Robin. I draw maps of places I've walked.

I spent nine seasons on trail crews and survey teams in the Columbia Mountains, then moved indoors (mostly) to draw them. Every line in my work has been walked, checked, and walked again, so the map still makes sense when the signal is gone and the light is going.

01Revelstoke Trail Atlasself-published atlas2025+ 
         /\
    /\  /  \    /\
   /  \/    \__/  \
 _/  o--o--o   ... \_
~~~~~~~~~.......~~~~

Six day-hike sheets around town, from cedar valley floors to the alpine. I reissue it every spring.

Why
Friends kept handing visitors photocopied sketches. I wanted one honest set of maps I'd give my own family.
How
Walked all six routes twice, in June and again in October, to catch seasonal junctions and washouts.
After
Stocked in four local shops and the visitor centre. Search-and-rescue call-outs on two of the routes dropped the next summer.

Ski-touring ascents, avalanche terrain ratings and safe exits, for the Glacier Backcountry Society.

Why
Six faded panels that nobody could read in a snowstorm, which is exactly when people need them.

Simplified the terrain ratings into three shapes with two forecasters, then tested drafts at the trailheads with gloves on.
After
Up for the 2024 to 2025 season, and now the template for the society's next four sites.

03The Monashees, Slowguidebook maps2024+

~~ .  .  .  .  .  . ~~

A four-day traverse split into kind stages, with water sources and bail-outs marked by hand. Published by Kootenay Press.

Why
People usually rush this traverse. The publisher and I both wanted a book that felt unhurried.
How
Eight spreads, one per half day, with hand-lettered notes wherever I'd have wanted a friend to warn me.
After
A second printing in under a year, and a nomination for a regional book design award.
  • book design
  • hand lettering
  • route research
04Basin wayfindingtrail system2023+ 
  [P]-----+-----[*]
        |    /\
   ..   +---/  \
       /   /    \
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Junction markers and you-are-here maps for 40 km of connected community trails in the Columbia Basin.

Why
Three volunteer groups, three trail systems, three sign styles. Visitors got lost exactly at the seams.
How
One symbol set, one colour per difficulty, and every junction numbered so a phone call can say "I'm at 14".
After
Wrong-turn reports at the shared junctions fell by more than half in the first season.

  • systems
  • Illustrator

05 · About me

About me

“A map is a promise somebody makes about a place they have been. I try to only promise things I have stood on.”

I work from one room above a bakery in Revelstoke. Most weeks are split between the drafting desk and a trail somewhere, checking a junction or a creek crossing that changed over the winter.

I came to maps sideways: through trail work, then survey work, then years of drawing every hike in the back of a notebook until people started asking for copies.

  • favourite ridgeKeystone Standard Basin rim, around 7 am
  • co-workerContour, border collie, has walked every route here
  • fuelwhatever the bakery downstairs burns first
  • worst mistakea creek on the wrong side of a ridge, 2021. It's why I proof on foot.
