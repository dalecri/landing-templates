I record the flora of the Southern Appalachians in ink and pigment, then bottle the same plants into seasonal remedies. Each study begins as a field sketch pressed between sheets of handmade paper.

Pressed Specimen Layering

The technique that organizes this page is the same one used in the studio: multiple translucent studies of a single plant are arranged with slight rotation, then pressed flat so the overlapping lines remain visible. Scientific accuracy and the living gesture of the plant coexist on one sheet.

On the page the four works begin slightly askew. The single control settles them into final alignment and draws the botanical lines in sage—the color of the mountain understory after rain.
