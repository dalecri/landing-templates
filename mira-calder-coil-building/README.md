# Mira Calder — Coil Building, Santa Fe

A stoneware potter's portfolio you build the way she builds: coil by coil, then fired.

## The persona (fictional)

**Mira Calder**, a ceramicist in Santa Fe, New Mexico, hand-building functional stoneware and quiet sculptural forms from local clay, wood-fired in the high desert. Three pieces stand for the work: a Desert Pitcher in ash glaze, a Coil Bowl with its joins left visible as texture, and a Tall Vase of five coils reduced in the wood kiln. Commissions and studio visits by arrangement; contact is a placeholder address (studio@example.com). Mira and her work are invented for this template.

## How the page works

- `index.html` is the whole template in one file (10,522 bytes). Only external calls: Google Fonts and the Open-Meteo fetch for live Santa Fe conditions (35.6870, -105.9378), shown in Fahrenheit as befits the place. Local-first, no accounts, no tracking.
- **Coil building:** the featured technique is the page's structure. A vessel at the top of the page starts as nothing; the visitor adds coils one at a time (Base, Body, Shoulder, Neck, Rim, five in all), then fires it, then resets and builds again. The page is layered the way the pots are.
- Type note: this build sets its text in Cormorant Garamond and Inter rather than Space Mono, a departure from the shelf's usual house style, kept as the builder delivered it.
